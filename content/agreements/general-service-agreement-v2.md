This Service Agreement ("Agreement") governs the provision of services by the Service Provider to the Client in connection with the proposal identified in Schedule 2 and accepted by the Client through the Service Provider's online proposal and agreement portal (the "Portal").

## 1. Parties

**1.1** The Service Provider is [[provider.name]], [[provider.role]][[provider.affiliation]], of [[provider.address]] ([[provider.email]]).

**1.2** The Client is the individual, business, organisation, or other entity identified in the accepted Proposal and represented by the person electronically signing this Agreement.

**1.3** Together, the Service Provider and Client are referred to as the "Parties."

## 2. Agreement and Incorporated Proposal

**2.1** This Agreement must be read together with the proposal accepted by the Client through the Portal ("Proposal"). "Proposal" means the specific version the Client accepted, identified by its version reference and acceptance date in Schedule 2.

**2.2** The Proposal is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement.

**2.3** The Proposal defines the project-specific commercial and operational terms, which may include:

- scope of work and deliverables;
- project phases and milestones;
- fees and pricing;
- payment schedule;
- project timeline;
- revisions and review periods;
- hosting, infrastructure, domain, licensing, or third-party service arrangements;
- support and maintenance;
- assumptions, exclusions, and dependencies; and
- any other terms specifically applicable to the engagement.

**2.4** This Agreement, any Special Terms in Schedule 1, and the accepted Proposal together constitute the contract between the Parties.

**2.5** Where there is an inconsistency between this Agreement, the Special Terms, and the Proposal:

1. a Special Term in Schedule 1 will apply to the provision it names;
2. a provision in the Proposal expressly stated to modify a provision of this Agreement will apply to that project;
3. project-specific commercial terms in the Proposal will apply to the relevant project; and
4. in all other cases, this Agreement will prevail.

## 3. Services

**3.1** The Service Provider will provide the services and deliverables described in the accepted Proposal with reasonable professional skill and care.

**3.2** Any service, feature, deliverable, integration, modification, or other work not included in the accepted Proposal is outside the agreed scope unless subsequently approved as a change request.

## 4. Commencement

**4.1** Unless otherwise stated in the Proposal, work will commence after:

- the Client has accepted the Proposal;
- this Agreement has been signed by both Parties;
- any required initial payment has been received; and
- the Client has provided any information, content, credentials, access, approvals, or materials reasonably required to begin the work.

**4.2** Any stated delivery date or timeline may depend on completion of these requirements.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

**5.1** The Client agrees to cooperate reasonably with the Service Provider and to provide information, materials, content, access, approvals, feedback, personnel, and decisions reasonably required for delivery of the services.

**5.2** The Client is responsible for ensuring that materials and information supplied to the Service Provider are accurate and that the Client has the necessary rights or permissions for their use.

**5.3** Where the Proposal specifies response or delivery periods for Client materials, those periods will apply.

**5.4** Client delays may result in corresponding adjustments to milestones, delivery dates, dependencies, resource allocation, or project schedules.

**5.5** A significant or prolonged Client delay may require the project schedule to be re-established based on the Service Provider's availability.

## 6. Fees and Payment

**6.1** The Client agrees to pay the fees specified in the Proposal according to the payment schedule stated there.

**6.2** Unless otherwise specified:

- invoices become due according to the payment terms stated in the Proposal or invoice;
- work may be paused where an amount remains overdue;
- the Service Provider is not responsible for project delays caused by payment suspension; and
- any agreed late-payment charges stated in the Proposal may be applied to overdue amounts.

**6.3** The Client remains responsible for fees for work properly performed before any suspension or termination.

**6.4** Third-party fees, subscriptions, licences, infrastructure charges, transaction fees, advertising expenditure, hosting charges, domain renewals, or similar expenses are payable as described in the Proposal.

## 7. Changes to Scope

**7.1** A request that changes the agreed scope, functionality, deliverables, assumptions, requirements, workload, integrations, or other material aspect of the project may be treated as a change request.

**7.2** Before performing material additional work, the Service Provider may provide:

- a description of the proposed change;
- any additional fee;
- any effect on the schedule; and
- any revised assumptions or dependencies.

**7.3** A change becomes part of the engagement when approved by the Client in writing or electronically through the Portal, email, or another mutually accepted communication channel.

**7.4** Minor implementation decisions reasonably necessary to deliver the agreed scope do not constitute scope changes.

## 8. Review, Testing and Acceptance

**8.1** Where the Proposal provides a review, testing, approval, or acceptance period, the Client must review the relevant deliverable within that period.

**8.2** The Client must identify any material failure to conform to the agreed scope with sufficient detail for the Service Provider to investigate.

**8.3** The Service Provider will correct verified defects relating to the agreed scope within a reasonable period.

**8.4** Requests for new functionality, changes in preference, additional revisions, or requirements outside the agreed scope are not defects and may be handled as change requests.

**8.5** Where a review period is specified and the Client does not provide feedback within that period, the deliverable may be considered accepted unless otherwise agreed.

**8.6** Use of a delivered system or deliverable in live operations may also constitute acceptance where it is reasonable to infer that the Client has accepted it for operational use.

## 9. Intellectual Property and Ownership

**9.1** Unless the Proposal provides otherwise, ownership of project-specific final deliverables created for the Client transfers to the Client only after all amounts due for the applicable work have been paid in full.

**9.2** The Service Provider retains ownership of:

- pre-existing software, tools, libraries, frameworks, templates, utilities, methods, know-how, processes, components, and intellectual property;
- reusable or general-purpose code and components not created exclusively for the Client; and
- improvements to the Service Provider's general tools, methods, systems, or processes.

**9.3** Where retained Service Provider materials are incorporated into a Client deliverable, the Client receives a perpetual licence to use those materials as reasonably necessary to use the delivered product.

**9.4** Third-party software, open-source components, platforms, APIs, fonts, libraries, services, media, and other third-party materials remain subject to their respective licences and terms.

**9.5** Unless otherwise agreed in the Proposal, the Service Provider may identify completed work as part of a professional portfolio or case study, provided confidential Client information is not disclosed.

## 10. Client Materials

**10.1** The Client retains ownership of materials, trademarks, content, data, branding, photographs, documents, and other intellectual property supplied by the Client.

**10.2** The Client grants the Service Provider permission to use those materials as reasonably necessary to provide the agreed services.

**10.3** The Client represents that it has the rights necessary to provide those materials for the intended use.

## 11. Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

**11.1** The services may depend on third-party products or services, including hosting providers, cloud platforms, domain registrars, email providers, payment processors, APIs, advertising platforms, software libraries, analytics services, social networks, external databases, or other technology providers.

**11.2** Unless expressly stated otherwise, the Service Provider does not control those third parties and is not responsible for:

- outages or interruptions caused by them;
- changes to their pricing, functionality, policies, APIs, eligibility requirements, or terms;
- suspension or termination of third-party accounts;
- loss caused solely by a third-party platform failure outside the Service Provider's reasonable control; or
- continued availability of a free or paid third-party service.

**11.3** The Service Provider will use reasonable care when configuring and integrating third-party services within the agreed scope.

## 12. Hosting, Domains and Recurring Services

**12.1** Hosting, domain registration, maintenance, infrastructure, software subscriptions, managed services, and other recurring services will be governed by the arrangements stated in the Proposal.

**12.2** Where the Service Provider manages such services on the Client's behalf, continued availability may depend on timely renewal and payment.

**12.3** Failure to pay an applicable renewal or recurring service charge may result in suspension, expiry, or interruption of the affected service.

**12.4** The Service Provider is not required to fund recurring Client costs after any period expressly included in the Proposal has expired.

## 13. Support and Maintenance

**13.1** Any warranty, support, maintenance, monitoring, training, or post-launch assistance included with the engagement is limited to the period and scope stated in the Proposal.

**13.2** Unless otherwise agreed, support does not include:

- new functionality;
- material changes to existing functionality;
- redesigns;
- work resulting from Client modifications or third-party changes;
- work arising from use outside the intended environment; or
- services outside the original project scope.

**13.3** Continued support after the included period may require a separate agreement or service plan.

## 14. Confidentiality

**14.1** Each Party may receive confidential or commercially sensitive information belonging to the other Party.

**14.2** Each Party agrees to:

- use confidential information only as reasonably necessary for the engagement;
- take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised disclosure; and
- not disclose it to third parties except where necessary for the project, authorised by the other Party, or required by law.

**14.3** Confidential information does not include information that:

- is publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
- was already lawfully known to the receiving Party;
- is lawfully obtained from another source without a confidentiality obligation; or
- is independently developed without reliance on the confidential information.

## 15. Data and Credentials

**15.1** Where the Client provides access credentials, account permissions, personal data, operational data, or other sensitive information, the Service Provider will use them only as reasonably necessary to perform the services.

**15.2** The Client remains responsible for maintaining appropriate administrative control over its accounts and for changing or revoking credentials where reasonably appropriate after handover.

**15.3** Where the project involves processing personal or confidential data, the Parties will cooperate in implementing reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the project and the agreed technology.

## 16. Warranties and Project Outcomes

**16.1** The Service Provider will use reasonable professional care in delivering the agreed services.

**16.2** Unless expressly stated in the Proposal, the Service Provider does not guarantee any particular commercial result, including:

- revenue;
- sales;
- customer acquisition;
- website traffic;
- search-engine position;
- social-media growth;
- advertising performance;
- bookings;
- conversion rates; or
- other business outcomes dependent on factors beyond the Service Provider's control.

## 17. Limitation of Liability

**17.1** To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Service Provider's total aggregate liability arising from the engagement will not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client to the Service Provider under the applicable Proposal.

**17.2** Neither Party will be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential loss arising from the engagement, to the extent permitted by law.

**17.3** Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

## 18. Suspension

**18.1** The Service Provider may suspend work or affected services where:

- an undisputed payment is materially overdue;
- the Client fails to provide essential information, access, materials, or approvals;
- continuing the work would create a material security or legal risk;
- the Client requests a pause; or
- another circumstance makes continued performance temporarily impracticable.

**18.2** The Service Provider will, where reasonably practicable, notify the Client of the reason for suspension.

**18.3** Project schedules may be adjusted following a suspension.

## 19. Termination

**19.1** Either Party may terminate the engagement by giving the notice specified in the Proposal or, where no period is specified, seven days' written notice.

**19.2** Either Party may terminate immediately where the other Party commits a material breach that cannot reasonably be remedied, or fails to remedy a remediable material breach within a reasonable period after written notice.

**19.3** Upon termination:

- the Client must pay for work properly completed up to the termination date;
- approved expenses and outstanding third-party costs incurred for the project remain payable;
- the Service Provider will provide completed paid-for deliverables that are reasonably capable of delivery;
- ownership rights transfer only to the extent that applicable payments have been completed; and
- provisions intended by their nature to survive termination will continue to apply.

**19.4** Payments attributable to work already performed or milestones already delivered are non-refundable unless otherwise agreed.

## 20. Force Majeure

**20.1** Neither Party will be responsible for delay or failure caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including major infrastructure failure, natural disaster, widespread telecommunications failure, government action, civil disturbance, epidemic, pandemic, or material disruption of essential third-party services.

**20.2** The affected Party must take reasonable steps to reduce the impact of the event.

**20.3** Deadlines may be adjusted to account for the resulting delay.

## 21. Communications and Notices

**21.1** Routine project communications may take place through the Portal, email, or another communication channel used by the Parties for the engagement.

**21.2** Formal notices relating to suspension, material breach, termination, or disputes should be sent to the email addresses associated with the engagement unless another notice method has been agreed.

**21.3** An electronic communication recorded as successfully sent to the designated address will constitute written notice, subject to applicable law.

## 22. Dispute Resolution

**22.1** The Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct discussion.

**22.2** If the dispute cannot be resolved, the Parties may attempt mediation by a mutually agreed mediator.

**22.3** Where mediation does not resolve the dispute, either Party may pursue arbitration where the Parties agree to arbitration or may exercise any other remedy available under applicable law.

**22.4** Any arbitration agreed under this Agreement will be conducted in accordance with the applicable laws of Ghana.

## 23. Governing Law

**23.1** This Agreement and the incorporated Proposal are governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

## 24. Entire Agreement

**24.1** This Agreement, any Special Terms in Schedule 1, the accepted Proposal, and any subsequent written or electronically approved change requests constitute the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the engagement.

**24.2** They replace prior discussions, representations, negotiations, or communications relating to the same subject matter, except where expressly incorporated.

## 25. Amendments

**25.1** No material amendment to an active engagement will be effective unless agreed by both Parties in writing or through an electronic acceptance mechanism capable of recording that agreement.

**25.2** Updates to the Service Provider's standard terms will not retrospectively modify an Agreement already entered into unless both Parties agree to the change.

## 26. Severability

**26.1** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in effect to the extent permitted by law.

## 27. Waiver

**27.1** A failure or delay by either Party to enforce a right under this Agreement does not waive that right.

## 28. Assignment

**28.1** Neither Party may transfer its material rights or obligations under this Agreement to another person without the other Party's consent, except as part of a lawful business reorganisation or transfer where doing so does not materially prejudice the other Party.

## 29. Electronic Agreement and Signature

**29.1** The Parties agree that this Agreement may be formed, accepted, signed, stored, and retained electronically.

**29.2** The Service Provider signs first, by typing its name and confirming its signature through the Portal. That signature constitutes the Service Provider's offer to enter into this Agreement on the terms recorded, which the Client accepts by signing.

**29.3** By selecting **"Accept and Sign Agreement"**, the person signing confirms that:

1. they have been given access to this Agreement and the incorporated Proposal before acceptance;
2. they have reviewed and agree to both documents;
3. the information they provide in connection with the signature is accurate;
4. where signing for an organisation, they are authorised to bind that organisation;
5. typing their full name and selecting **"Accept and Sign Agreement"** is intended to constitute their electronic signature;
6. they consent to use of electronic records and electronic signatures for this engagement; and
7. they intend to be legally bound by the Agreement and incorporated Proposal.

**29.4** The electronic record generated by the Portal, including the identity information supplied by the signatory, the applicable document versions, date and time of signing, verification records, and associated audit information, will form part of the record of this Agreement.

## 30. Effective Date

**30.1** Unless otherwise stated in the Proposal, this Agreement becomes effective when the Client signs a version of it that the Service Provider has already signed.

**30.2** Where commencement is also conditional on an initial payment, performance obligations will begin after that payment has been received.
